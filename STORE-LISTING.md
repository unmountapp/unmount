## Full Description (4000 chars max)

UnMount turns breaking a bad habit into a visual, satisfying journey.

Every habit you want to quit becomes a mountain — looming, imposing, and real. Each day you stay clean, you chip away at it. Watch the mountain physically shrink, piece by piece, over 66 days of clean living.

Slip up? The mountain grows back a little. That's life. Get back up and keep chipping.

Science supports the 66-day framework — research published in the European Journal of Social Psychology found it takes an average of 66 days for a new behavior to become automatic. UnMount makes those 66 days feel achievable, one clean day at a time.

**WHY UNMOUNT WORKS**

• Visual progress is more motivating than numbers. Watching a mountain shrink is viscerally satisfying in a way that a streak counter never is.
• The relapse mechanic is forgiving. You don't lose everything when you slip — just a bit of progress. This reduces the "all-or-nothing" thinking that kills most habit-breaking attempts.
• One focus at a time (free) keeps you from spreading yourself too thin.
• No judgment. No shame. Just a mountain to chip away at.
