UnMount Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

UnMount ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the UnMount mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

UnMount is designed to work entirely on your device. The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • Your habit name and daily check-in logs
  • Your progress data (clean days, relapse count, streaks)
  • App preferences such as selected mountain theme

2. In-App Purchases

UnMount Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to manage subscription state. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase receipts to validate your subscription. No personally identifiable information is shared with RevenueCat beyond what Apple or Google provides as part of standard purchase validation.

For RevenueCat's privacy practices, visit: revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Analytics and Tracking

UnMount does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not track your behavior, sell your data, or share any information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. If you delete the app, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted. We have no ability to recover your data once it is deleted.

5. Children's Privacy

UnMount is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@unmountapp.com
